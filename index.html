Chapter 1 · Curiosity

It started with a simple question during my B.Tech in Electronics & Communication at AKTU: what actually happens inside a chip? Circuits on paper weren't enough, so I chased the answer outside the classroom too, through NPTEL's Digital Circuits course and industrial training at BRBRAITT.

Chapter 2 · Going deep

That question took me to IIT Bhubaneswar for an M.Tech in Semiconductor Technology & Chip Design, on a Samsung fellowship through the ISWDP program with Synopsys and IISc. I learned to build devices one layer at a time, simulating them in Sentaurus TCAD, measuring power MOSFETs across temperature in the lab, and working on MoS₂ photodiodes and gas sensors.

Chapter 3 · Something new

For my research I took on a hard problem in power semiconductor devices: stopping a 1.7 kV-class 4H-SiC superjunction diode from breaking down at its edges. I proposed a new wide-trench termination that needs no extra implant and still reaches about 95% of the ideal breakdown voltage. The project earned a perfect 10/10. The paper was accepted for oral presentation at PCIM India 2026, and a patent is being filed. Along the way I also taught, as a teaching assistant in the VLSI Design Lab and as an instructor at a chip design workshop.

Chapter 4 · Today

Since July 2026 I have been a Product Development Engineer at Sandisk, working on NAND device validation and reliability checks, where the device physics I learned meets products people use every day. The question from Chapter 1 hasn't changed. I just get to answer it at scale now.
