M.Tech research

A wide-trench termination for 4H-SiC charge-sheet superjunctions.

Edge terminations stop a power device from breaking down early at its edges. I proposed one for the 4H-SiC CSSJ that reuses the same trench and Al₂O₃/SiO₂ dielectric stack as the active cells, so it needs no extra termination implant.

Accepted for publication · PCIM India 2026, New Delhi

Selected for oral presentation at PCIM India 2026 (Power Conversion and Intelligent Motion), New Delhi. Authors: Amit Ranjan Sahu, Akshay K. A patent on the termination is in the process of filing.

TCAD based study of 4H-SiC charge sheet superjunctions with a wide-trench termination

2D Sentaurus TCAD simulations of a 1700 V-class CSSJ diode using the Hatakeyama impact-ionization model. Without a field plate, the termination stops at about 940 V because the field crowds at the trench corner. A field plate over the trench on a thin dielectric splits that peak between the corner and the plate edge.

Supervised by Dr. Akshay K and Dr. Sayan Dey, IIT Bhubaneswar. Supported by the PM Early Career Research Grant (ANRF).
